CALCLAB / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

A clear, simple explanation of how CalcLab handles information.

CalcLab’s tools run in your browser. The calculator does not send your expressions or results to a server, and this site does not require an account.

Information and storage

Search terms are used only in your browser to filter the tools shown on the page. The current version of CalcLab does not intentionally store the information you enter into its tools.

Third-party services

This site loads its Manrope and DM Mono fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may share standard connection information with that service when requesting those fonts.

Questions

For questions or feedback, contact the CalcLab project on GitHub.
